Qiraa’h

Do

  • Eligibility / categories: Ages 6–8: Surah Al Adiyat; 9–12: Surah Al Burooj; 13–16: Surah Al Mutaffifeen; 17+: Surah Al-Mursalat.
  • Age 16 is assigned to the 13–16 category only.

Don't

  • Memorization is not mandatory.
  • Participants are judged on Tajweed and Qiraa’h.
  • Qiraa’h and Hifz cannot be combined.

Hifz (Memorization)

Do

  • Eligibility / categories: Beginner: Surah Waqiah and Surah Hashr.
  • Intermediate: Surah Qasas and Surah Ankabuth.
  • Advanced: Surah Yusuf, Surah Isra, and Surah Kahf.

Don't

  • Choose exactly one level.
  • Cannot also enter Qiraa’h.

Adhan

Do

  • Full Adhan with proper tone and Tajweed. 40% Makharij-ul-Huruf and clarity; 60% melody.
  • Eligibility / categories: Only boys ages 9–16.
  • Category 1: ages 9–12 on poster; Category 2: ages 13–16.
  • Age 9 is eligible for Category 1.

Cook-Off

Do

  • Registration is mandatory. $5 entry fee per person according to the Cook-Off rules image.
  • Each participant must submit a separate registration.
  • Prepare your dish at home; pre-cooked food only.
  • Bring your prepared dish, safe packaging, and an ingredient/allergen disclosure.
  • Judging covers presentation, taste, creativity and originality.
  • Eligibility / categories: Elementary: Mini Chef Dessert Clash (8+).
  • Middle & High School: Street Food.
  • Adults: Culinary Showcase / Bring Your Heritage Food.
  • Registration categories use age bands for elementary and grade for middle/high; final divisions should be confirmed by organizers.

Drawing

Do

  • Topic: Stories of our Prophet.
  • Bring your own art supplies.
  • Eligibility / categories: Kindergarten through 5th grade.

3D Family Art

Do

  • Theme: The Bounty of Allah.
  • Must be three-dimensional, not a flat painting or drawing.
  • Student must complete the artwork with family collaboration.
  • Recycled household materials encouraged.
  • Prepare a 1–2 minute spoken explanation video; include a small title card with family name and artwork title.
  • Eligibility / categories: Middle and high school students.

Islamic Speech Competition

Do

  • Arrival: Report to the registration desk by 9:00 AM sharp , sit in the designated area and follow the assigned speaking order.
  • Late arrivals may not be accommodated.
  • School categories & time limits: Elementary School — 2 minutes ; Middle School — 3 minutes ; High School — 5 minutes .
  • The timekeeper gives a warning with approximately 30 seconds remaining.
  • Overtime may affect scoring.
  • Registration categories use school grade (K–5, 6–8, 9–12 respectively).
  • Judging (100 points): Islamic content and understanding — 30; confidence and presentation — 20; voice clarity and expression — 20; organization and flow — 15; time management — 15.
  • Coordinator: Ummu Khuldhum · 732-983-6179. Speech WhatsApp group.

Don't

  • Speech preparation: Prepare an age-appropriate Islamic topic.
  • Speak individually, clearly and confidently.
  • Relevant Quranic verses, authentic Hadith and practical examples are encouraged.
  • Avoid reading directly from a script.
  • Materials: Handwritten notes or cue cards are allowed; preparation must be finished before the contest.
  • No mobile phones, smartwatches, tablets, laptops, internet or other electronic devices during the competition.
  • Conduct: Dress modestly, respect other speakers, do not interrupt or prompt, and remain at the venue until the category ends.
  • Judges’ decisions are final.

Islamic Essay Writing Competition

Do

  • Arrival: Arrive by 9:00 AM sharp ; register and be seated before writing starts.
  • Late arrivals may not receive extra time.
  • School categories & word limits: Middle School — 400–600 words ; High School — 700–1,000 words .
  • Middle school essays should show Islamic knowledge and personal reflection; high school essays should show deeper analysis, critical thinking and connections to contemporary life.
  • Registration categories use school grade (6–8 and 9–12 respectively).
  • Judging criteria: Relevance to topic; understanding of Islamic teachings; depth of thought and personal reflection; originality and creativity; organization, clarity and language; grammar, spelling and presentation.
  • Coordinator: Ummu Khuldhum · 732-983-6179. Essay Writing WhatsApp group.

Don't

  • Provided materials: Paper, pencil, eraser and pen.
  • Participants may bring additional stationery.
  • Electronic devices are strictly prohibited , including phones, tablets, laptops and smartwatches.
  • No internet or digital notes.
  • Writing rules: Write independently, in your own words, on the assigned topic only.
  • Relevant Quranic verses, Hadith and Islamic examples are welcome.
  • Plagiarism and copying are prohibited.
  • Maintain silence, write legibly, follow coordinator instructions and submit your essay before leaving.
